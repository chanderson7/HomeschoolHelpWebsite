HOMESCHOOLHELPER · HELP & LEGAL

Support

Help for your homeschool day, your account, and your records.

HomeSchoolHelper is in development for iOS 17 and later. The browser prototype is an earlier design study and does not save real records.

Contact

The current app lists support@homeschoolhelper.app for support and privacy@homeschoolhelper.app for privacy concerns.

Include your app version, iOS version, device model, the steps that led to the problem, and any error message. Remove learner names and records from screenshots. Never send passwords or confirmation/reset links.

Signing in

Register with your email address and password. If asked to confirm your email, open the confirmation message and return to the app. Use the login screen's password-reset option if needed. Account verification requires an internet connection; check connectivity if the app cannot verify an existing session.

Planning for your family

Add a learner in Family, then build a lesson sequence in Curriculum & Plan. A shared sequence can create independent assignments for multiple learners. Choose teaching weekdays or flexible, undated work. Update a lesson's status or date, or move overdue unfinished work to today.

Attendance and learning activities

Confirm instructional time explicitly. Completing a lesson or logging a shared activity does not automatically add attendance. Saving attendance again for the same learner and date updates that day's total. Example annual targets are not a determination of local homeschool requirements.

Backups and restore

Go to Settings → Manage Backups & Restore. Choose Back up current records and confirm to upload a snapshot. Refresh to see recent backups. Restoring a selected snapshot replaces current local records, so back up changes you want to keep first. This is manual backup and restore, not live device synchronization.

If records fail to load or save

Do not uninstall as a first troubleshooting step: records may exist only on this device. Note the error, check device storage, and contact support. The app blocks edits after a failed load and retains the last saved state when saving fails.

Privacy, deletion, and terms

Read our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Data & account choices. Account and cloud-backup deletion controls are not yet available in the reviewed development build. Signing out does not delete your data.

Public bug reports

Technical issues can also be reported in the app repository or website repository. GitHub issues are public and may require a GitHub account. Use email for account-specific concerns and never post private family information.
