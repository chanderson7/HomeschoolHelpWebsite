Data & account choices

Development version · Updated September 25, 2026. These pages describe the current development build. Account deletion and the final retention schedule are not yet implemented or finalized. This is not an announcement of App Store availability.

Local records, cloud snapshots, and your account are separate. Changing one does not automatically remove the others.

Choose whether to upload records

Creating or updating a lesson saves it locally. A cloud snapshot is uploaded only after you choose Settings → Manage Backups & Restore → Back up current records and confirm. You can leave cloud backup unused; email authentication still connects to Supabase.

Restore carefully

Open Manage Backups & Restore, refresh the list, select a backup, and review the replacement warning. Restoring replaces this account's local records. It does not merge records or update another device. The list shows the most recent 20 backups and does not establish a retention limit.

Sign out or reset a password

Use Settings → Sign Out to end access on this device. Signing out does not delete records or your account. For a forgotten password, use the password-reset option on the login screen and follow the email link back to the app.

Remove local app files

Deleting the app through iOS removes its local app files; offloading preserves app data. Keep any important information before deleting. Cloud snapshots, account data, and copies in device backups are separate. Keychain credentials can also have a different lifecycle from app files, so sign out first when possible.

Account and cloud deletion: current limitation

The current development build does not offer an account-deletion button or a cloud-backup deletion button. This page is informational and does not submit a deletion request. Do not interpret uninstalling, signing out, or stopping backups as deleting an account.

For privacy concerns, the current app lists privacy@homeschoolhelper.app. Send a general description from your account email without passwords, recovery codes, or learner records. No automatic deletion or completion deadline is promised here. An in-app deletion flow must be completed before App Store submission.

Access and correction

Review your learners, plans, attendance, and activities in the app. Use the available editing controls to correct records. Local changes do not rewrite older snapshots. The reviewed app does not include a user-facing JSON, PDF, or transcript export control.

Read the Privacy Policy for data types, providers, and retention details, or visit Support.
