Privacy Policy

Who and what this covers

This policy describes the HomeSchoolHelper iOS app and this companion website. The app is intended for parents, guardians, and authorized educators managing learning records. References to HomeSchoolHelper mean the team providing this app and website. The website demo is a separate design study, not the native application.

Information handled by the app

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, authentication credentials, and session information. Email and password requests go to Supabase Authentication for registration and sign-in. Confirmation and password-reset emails support account access.
  • Learner information: names and grade levels entered by an adult, with identifiers linking records to each learner.
  • Learning records: course and lesson titles, lesson sequences, assignments, scheduled and completion dates, assignment status, attendance dates and instructional minutes, and activity titles, dates, and minutes.
  • Preferences: settings such as appearance, default schedule, and haptic feedback are stored on the device.
  • Support information: information you choose to include in an email or issue report. Please provide only what is needed to explain the problem.

The reviewed build contains no advertising or behavioral analytics SDK, tracking-based student profiles, purchases, or receipt/photo uploads. HomeSchoolHelper does not sell family records or use them for advertising.

Why this information is used

Account information authenticates access and supports recovery. Learning records power daily checklists, curriculum plans, progress, attendance, and activity history. A backup stores a recoverable copy when you request one. Support information is used to investigate your request. Authentication and hosting providers may also process technical request and security logs to operate their services.

Local storage and account access

Learning records are saved as JSON in the app's local storage, in a separate file for each signed-in account. Authentication sessions use the SDK's Keychain-backed storage. Local saving does not automatically upload school records.

The current app checks account access with the authentication service when starting or becoming active. An internet connection may therefore be needed to enter the app even though learning records are stored locally. Signing out does not erase the local school-record files or delete cloud backups.

Optional cloud backups

In Settings → Manage Backups & Restore, choosing “Back up current records” and confirming the upload sends a snapshot of your current learning records to Supabase. It includes learner names and grade levels, lessons, assignments, attendance, and activities, linked to your account ID and the backup creation time.

Backups are manual snapshots. They are not continuous synchronization and do not automatically merge changes between devices. Restoring replaces the current account's local records with the selected snapshot. Earlier backups can contain information no longer present in your current records.

The backup schema restricts ordinary client access to the signed-in owner using row-level security. This is an account access control, not end-to-end encryption: authorized service operators may have administrative access. The app connects to its service over HTTPS. No storage or transmission method provides an absolute security guarantee.

Service providers and disclosure

Supabase supplies authentication and cloud backup infrastructure. Its services process account data and any backup you elect to upload; service and email-delivery infrastructure may process connection information such as IP addresses, timestamps, and security events. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Apple's device and backup services may hold app data according to your device settings. Website hosting, font, library-delivery, and support providers are described below. Provider processing can occur outside your location; this site does not promise a particular storage country or region.

Information may also need to be disclosed when legally required, to address abuse or security incidents, or at your direction. Do not upload records belonging to a family you are not authorized to represent.

Retention and deletion

Local files remain until removed from the device. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app files; it does not remove cloud accounts or backups, and device backups may retain copies under your Apple settings.

The current cloud-backup implementation has no automatic expiration schedule. The history view lists up to 20 recent backups; this is a display limit, not a promise that older copies are deleted. Signing out and restoring a snapshot do not delete older cloud snapshots.

The current development build does not include an account-deletion or individual cloud-backup deletion control. A final retention schedule for account records, cloud snapshots, infrastructure recovery copies, and service logs has not yet been established. We do not claim immediate deletion or a fixed deletion deadline.

See Data & account choices for the controls currently available. Contact the privacy address below about access, correction, or deletion concerns. An email inquiry is not an automated deletion mechanism.

Children's and learners' privacy

The app is designed for adults, not child account holders. Adults may enter information about learners only when authorized to do so. Use the minimum identifying information necessary; avoid sensitive health information or unrelated private details in lesson and activity titles.

Learner information stays local unless included in an adult-requested cloud snapshot. There are no public learner profiles, student messaging, or advertising features in the reviewed build. If you believe a child has provided information without appropriate adult involvement, contact the privacy address without attaching educational records.

Your requests and choices

You can choose not to create cloud snapshots. You can update records where the app provides an editing control, reset your password, and sign out. Earlier cloud snapshots are not updated when local records change. A user-facing JSON or transcript export is not available in the reviewed build.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restrictions on processing, and to raise concerns with a privacy regulator. Send a general request to the privacy contact below; do not send passwords, recovery codes, identity documents, or learner records. Identity may need to be verified before information is disclosed or changed.

Website and browser prototype

The website itself has no sign-in, signup form, payments, or learning-record database. It does not set cookies or include visitor analytics or advertising trackers. The illustrative browser prototype uses fictional records and temporary page memory; reloading resets changes. Use sample information only.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) and the prototype loads interface and icon libraries from unpkg.com. These requests send ordinary connection information to the providers. Read Google's privacy policy. Blocking external resources may change fonts or icons but does not create an app account.

The public website address configured in the app uses Netlify. Hosting services may process request logs including IP addresses, requested files, timestamps, and browser information. See Netlify's privacy policy. Browser history and caching are controlled through your browser settings.

GitHub links lead to a separate service governed by GitHub's privacy statement. Public issues and screenshots are visible to others. Do not post family records there.

Privacy contact and updates

The privacy address listed in the current app is privacy@homeschoolhelper.app. For general help, see Support.

This policy will be revised as the app's features and operating practices change. The date above identifies this version. New account, sharing, billing, or tracking features require corresponding disclosures before use.
