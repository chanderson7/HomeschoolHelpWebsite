A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I download the app today?+

Not yet. HomeSchoolHelper is currently a design prototype and development plan. You can explore the concept here while the native iOS app is being developed.

Can I plan for more than one child?+

Yes—multi-child planning is part of the proposed core experience. The prototype illustrates individual child filters and a shared household overview.

What happens when our schedule changes?+

The planned workflow lets you review proposed moves for unfinished lessons before applying them. The prototype demonstrates this flow with sample data; it does not calculate real schedule changes.

Will I be able to export records and transcripts?+

Record and transcript exports are planned. The current prototype shows sample previews, but does not generate PDFs or save educational records.

What will it cost?+

Pricing has not been finalized. Any prices shown inside the prototype are exploratory, not a commercial offer.

Which devices will it support?+

HomeSchoolHelper is being planned for iOS. Supported devices and the minimum iOS version are still being decided.
