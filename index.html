YOUR RECORDS, WITH A RECOVERY OPTION

Saved here.
Backed up by you.

Learning records save on your device for your signed-in account. When you choose, upload a private snapshot or restore an earlier one from Account & backups. Changes do not sync automatically between devices.

Account access requires online verification. Restoring replaces local records; signing out does not delete them.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I download the app today?+

A native iOS app is in development, with accounts, local records, and manual cloud backups. No public App Store download is announced on this site. The browser preview is an earlier design study.

Can I plan for more than one child?+

Yes. The current native build supports learner profiles, shared lesson sequences with independent assignments, child filters, and multi-learner activity entries.

What happens when our schedule changes?+

The current native app lets you change lesson dates and move overdue unfinished lessons to today. Advanced rescheduling previews and guarded undo are still planned.

Will I be able to export records and transcripts?+

Record and transcript exports are planned. The current prototype shows sample previews, but does not generate PDFs or save educational records.

What will it cost?+

Pricing has not been finalized. Any prices shown inside the prototype are exploratory, not a commercial offer.

Which devices will it support?+

The current native app targets iOS 17 and later. Final App Store device availability will be listed when the app is released.
