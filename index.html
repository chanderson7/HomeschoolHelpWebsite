YOUR RECORDS, WITH A RECOVERY OPTION

Saved here.
Protected with choices.

Learning records save on your device for your signed-in account. Automatic cloud backup is enabled by default in the current development build and can be paused in Account & backups; you can also save or restore a snapshot manually.

Account access still requires online verification. Cloud backup stores successive snapshots rather than merging live edits between devices. Restoring replaces local records; signing out does not delete them.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I download the app today?+

A native iOS app is in development. No public App Store download is announced on this site. The browser preview is an earlier design study.

Can I plan for more than one child?+

Yes. The source supports multiple learner profiles and shared lessons. The development subscription configuration limits the free plan to one learner and unlocks additional learners with Pro.

What happens when our schedule changes?+

The current source includes individual date changes, moving overdue work to today, paced rescheduling previews, conflict handling, and undo for eligible changes.

Can I export records and transcripts?+

Yes. The native source generates PDF reports and transcripts, CSV exports, calendar files, report cards, and reading logs. The browser prototype does not generate real files.

What will it cost?+

The development configuration includes a free plan limited to one learner and optional Pro subscriptions at $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year, with a seven-day annual trial where offered. Final availability, eligibility, tax, currency, and price are shown by Apple at purchase.

Which devices will it support?+

The current app targets iOS 17 and later and includes compact and regular-width layouts. Final App Store compatibility will be listed when released.
